  </PresentationStep>

  <PresentationStep effect="fade-up" hold={2200}>
    <div class="not-prose space-y-1">
      <Presentation_eqbox label="SAC — Actor-Critic Objective">
        $$\mathcal{J}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{s,\,\epsilon}\bigl[Q(s,a) - \alpha\log\pi_\theta(a\mid s)\bigr]$$
      </Presentation_eqbox>
      <Presentation_eqnote tag="BPTT" tagTone="red">
        $a = \pi_\theta(a\mid s, \epsilon)$ · $\frac{\partial a}{\partial \theta}$ requires backprop through all ODE steps
      </Presentation_eqnote>
    </div>
  </PresentationStep>

  <PresentationStep effect="fade-up" hold={2200}>
    <div class="not-prose mt-2 space-y-1">
      <Presentation_eqbox label="PPO — Policy Gradient">
        $$\mathcal{J}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_t\bigl[\min\bigl(r_t(\theta)\hat{A}_t,\;\mathrm{clip}(r_t,1{-}\varepsilon,1{+}\varepsilon)\hat{A}_t\bigr)\bigr], \qquad r_t(\theta) = \frac{\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)}{\pi_{\theta_{k}}(a_t\mid s_t)}$$
      </Presentation_eqbox>
      <Presentation_eqnote tag="BPTT" tagTone="red">
        $\log \pi_\theta(a\mid s)$ · change-of-variables — $\frac{\partial \log \pi_\theta(a\mid s)}{\partial \theta}$ requires backprop through ODE Jacobian
      </Presentation_eqnote>
    </div>
  </PresentationStep>

  <PresentationStep effect="fade" hold={800}>
    <PresentationCallout tone="red" label="Problem">
      ❌ Both gradient-based approaches require BPTT through ODE generation — numerically unstable and memory inefficient.
    </PresentationCallout>
  </PresentationStep>

</PresentationSection>

      </Presentation_eqbox>
      <Presentation_eqnote tag="No BPTT" tagTone="teal">

  <PresentationStep effect="fade-up" hold={2000}>
    <div class="not-prose grid gap-2 sm:grid-cols-2">

        <p class="text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-amber-600 dark:text-amber-400">MaxEnt Target</p>
        <p class="mt-1 font-mono text-sm text-amber-900 dark:text-amber-200">q*(a|s) ∝ exp(Q / α)</p>
        <p class="mt-2 text-xs text-amber-700 dark:text-amber-300">⚠️ Q is a learned critic — partition function intractable</p>
      </div>
      <div class="rounded-xl border border-amber-200 bg-amber-50 px-4 py-3 dark:border-amber-800/50 dark:bg-amber-900/20">
        <p class="text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-amber-600 dark:text-amber-400">Mirror Descent Target</p>
        <p class="mt-1 font-mono text-sm text-amber-900 dark:text-amber-200">q_k ∝ π_k · exp(Q / λ_k)</p>
        <p class="mt-2 text-xs text-amber-700 dark:text-amber-300">⚠️ Unnormalized — same issue</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </PresentationStep>

  <PresentationStep effect="fade-up" hold={2000}>
    <div class="not-prose mt-2 space-y-1">
      <Presentation_eqbox label="Solution — Importance Sampling">
        $$\theta_{k+1}\approx\arg\max_\theta\;\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}w^{(i)}\log\pi_\theta(a^{(i)}\mid s),\quad a^{(i)}\sim\pi_k(\cdot\mid s)$$
        $$w^{(i)}\propto\exp\!\left(\frac{Q(s,a^{(i)})}{\lambda_k}\right)$$
      </Presentation_eqbox>
      <Presentation_eqnote>
        Sample from π_k, reweight by exp(Q / λ_k) — no direct sampling needed
      </Presentation_eqnote>
    </div>
  </PresentationStep>

<PresentationSection id="present-q1" tabHold={3500} holdAfter={1200}>
  <ppt_QCard number="Q1" title="How do we construct local IS, and is it consistent with the original MDP?">

    <Fragment slot="challenge">
    For local IS to be principled — not just a trick — two conditions must hold:

    - **Localization.** The proposal and target distributions must share a *region* indexed by a latent $z$.
    - **Consistency.** Optimizing inside that local region must be the *same* optimization problem as optimizing the RL objective in the original MDP.

    Without Consistency, local IS optimizes the wrong objective and any gains are illusory.
    </Fragment>

    <Fragment slot="solution">
    <ppt_IdeaTabs labels={["Policy Parameterization", "Latent-Augmented MDP", "Consistency"]}>
      <Fragment slot="tab-0">
        <Figure>
          <Picture slot="figure" src="../assets/policy_parameterization.pdf" alt="Flow map transports latent z to multi-modal anchor actions" />
          <Fragment slot="caption">The deterministic flow map $T_\theta(s, z)$ transports a latent prior $z \sim p_z$ to multi-modal anchor actions.</Fragment>
        </Figure>
      </Fragment>
      <Fragment slot="tab-1">
        <Figure>
          <Picture slot="figure" src="../assets/zMDP.pdf" alt="Graphical model of the latent-augmented MDP" />
          <Fragment slot="caption">The **z-MDP** augments each state with the latent $z$.</Fragment>
        </Figure>
      </Fragment>
      <Fragment slot="tab-2">
        The z-MDP preserves both **state–action marginals** and the **Q-function**.

        <div class="not-prose my-3 grid gap-4 md:grid-cols-2">
          <Presentation_eqbox label="Corollary 4.2 — Marginal consistency">
            $$
            \rho_\pi(s, a) = \int \hat\rho_{\hat\pi}(s, z, a)\,dz
            $$
          </Presentation_eqbox>
          <Presentation_eqbox label="Corollary 4.4 — Q-function consistency">
            $$
            Q^{\hat\pi}(\hat s, a) = Q^{\pi}(s, a), \quad \forall\, s, z, a
            $$
          </Presentation_eqbox>
        </div>
      </Fragment>
    </ppt_IdeaTabs>
    </Fragment>

    <Fragment slot="bottom-line">
    We construct the **z-MDP**, and prove that optimizing the RL objective on the z-MDP is **equivalent** to optimizing it on the original MDP (**Consistency**).
    </Fragment>

  </ppt_QCard>
</PresentationSection>
